# Galaxy Searchlight

The Milky Way seen from above. Your horizon slices the galaxy's disc through the Sun's position, and the half above it
lights up as a beam that sweeps round through the night. The Messier clusters sit at their real distances, with the
globular clusters gathered round the centre.

Live at https://cosmic-obs.github.io/searchlight/

The page (`index.html`) is published from the galaxy-searchlight project.
