# Galaxy Searchlight

The Milky Way in 3D. Your horizon is a sheet through the Sun's position that slices the galaxy's disc, and the half
above it lights up as a beam that sweeps round through the night. The Messier clusters sit at their real distances and
heights, with the globular clusters swarming round the centre. Turn, zoom and fly round it, or hold your horizon level
and watch the galaxy wheel round you.

Live at https://cosmic-obs.github.io/searchlight/

The page (`index.html`) is published from the galaxy-searchlight project.
